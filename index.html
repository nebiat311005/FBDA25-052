Struggles that shaped Eritrea

From colonisation and war to sanctions and migration, Eritrea has gone through many difficult periods. These events affected families, education and daily life.

Resilience in everyday life

Even with all these challenges, Eritreans continue to study, work, celebrate culture and support each other at home and in the diaspora.

Eritrean culture in focus

Culture is a big part of how Eritreans stay strong. Food, music, coffee ceremonies and traditional clothes connect people everywhere.

Coffee Ceremony

A slow and social coffee making process that shows hospitality. It is usually served with popcorn or bread.

Languages

Tigrinya, Tigre, Arabic and other languages are spoken, showing the diversity of the people.

Music & Dance

Traditional dances and songs, as well as modern music, tell stories about life, migration and hope.

Culture & Identity

Shared rituals that keep Eritrea connected

From coffee ceremonies to traditional music, Eritrean culture strengthens community and resilience, both at home and across the diaspora.

Coffee ceremony

Green coffee beans are washed, roasted, ground, and brewed in front of guests. The smell, sound, and taste make it a slow, intentional moment of connection.

Food

Injera with tsebhi (stews), himbasha (sweet bread), and shiro are common dishes. Meals are often shared from the same tray, symbolising unity and care.

Languages & faith

Tigrinya, Tigre, Arabic and other languages, along with Christian and Muslim traditions, shape local customs, celebrations, and holidays.

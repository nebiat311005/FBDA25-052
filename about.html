About This Project

About the Eritrea Website

This page explains why I created this website, who it is for and how it connects to the struggles and resilience of Eritrean people.

Purpose of the Website

I built this website as a student project to showcase Eritrea in a more personal way. Many people only hear about Eritrea through short news stories, so I wanted to put history, culture, daily life and personal memories in one place.

The main goal is to highlight both the struggles and the strength of the people. It is not an official website, but a small digital space where I can collect information, stories and visuals that matter to me and my community.

Target Audience

This website is mainly for:

  • Students and friends who want to learn basic facts about Eritrea.
  • Members of the Eritrean diaspora who miss home and want to see familiar images.
  • Anyone who is curious about the history, culture and resilience of Eritreans.

I tried to keep the language simple and the layout clean so that people with different technical skills can still use the site on phones, tablets and laptops.

How the Pages Fit Together

Each page has a small role:

  • Home gives a quick overview and sets the tone.
  • History focuses on important events and struggles.
  • Culture shows traditions, food, music and daily life.
  • Resilience highlights how people keep going despite challenges.
  • Gallery shares visual impressions of places and people.
  • Archive keeps more personal stories from family and friends.
  • Feedback lets visitors send comments and suggestions.

Limitations & Future Ideas

Right now the site is simple and mostly static. In the future I would like to:

  • Add more real stories and media to the archive section.
  • Translate some pages into Tigrinya and Arabic for better access.
  • Use a small database or JSON file to manage stories instead of only HTML.
  • Improve accessibility and add more detailed timelines on the history page.

Even with these limits, this version already helps me practice web design and also keep a small digital memory of Eritrea.

About Eritrea Horizons

Eritrea Horizons is dedicated to telling Eritrean stories. It brings together history, culture, and diaspora memories so that Eritreans and friends of Eritrea can explore the country beyond headlines.

Website Objectives

  • Present Eritrea's history and culture in a respectful, positive way.
  • Offer Eritrean diaspora visitors a space to reflect on emotional stories.
  • Celebrate Eritrean resilience, creativity, and everyday life.
  • Encourage younger Eritreans to learn more about their roots.

Target Audience

This site is mainly created for:

  • Eritrean students and youth in the diaspora.
  • Non-Eritrean classmates who want to learn about the country.
  • Lecturers and tutors assessing this assignment.

Eritrean Identity and Diaspora

Many Eritreans live in cities such as London, Stockholm, Toronto, and Johannesburg while staying deeply connected to Asmara, Keren, Massawa, and their villages. This site acknowledges that connection and gives space to both pride and pain.

Future Development Ideas

  • Collect real-life stories from Eritreans in the diaspora.
  • Include audio clips of Eritrean music and interviews.
  • Translate key sections into Tigrinya, Arabic, and other languages.
  • Show interactive maps of Eritrean communities worldwide.
